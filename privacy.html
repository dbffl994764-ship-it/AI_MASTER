Privacy

Last updated 12 October 2026

The calculator

Everything you type into Agent Meter is calculated in your browser. It is not sent to us or stored on a server. The page remembers only your language choice, in your browser's local storage. We don't use analytics, ads or tracking cookies.

Fonts and hosting

The page is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its fonts from Google Fonts. Like any website request, those services receive your IP address and browser details under their own privacy policies.

Buying the playbook

If you buy the playbook, Lemon Squeezy collects your name, email and payment details to process the order, as merchant of record under its own privacy policy. We receive your name, email and order details to deliver the product and handle support or refunds. We don't sell this information or use it for marketing without asking.
